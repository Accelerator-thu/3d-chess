How to play

Goal: be first to place four stones in a straight line. Orange always moves first.

Winning lines

Lines can run along any axis, across any face diagonal, or through the cube's space diagonals — 13 directions in 3D. In 4D the board is N stacked cubes (w = 1…N) and lines may also run across cubes: 40 directions.

Views

Stack shows the layers in perspective: drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom. Layers lays each z-level side by side, flat — easier for spotting lines.

Gravity

You choose an (x, y) column and the stone falls to the lowest empty z, like Connect Four. Fixed for the whole game.

Blind mode

The board is hidden; type coordinates like 2,4,1 (1-based; x,y with gravity, plus w in 4D).

Online

Start a game and share the link. Moves travel directly between your browsers (WebRTC); a public server is only used to introduce you.
